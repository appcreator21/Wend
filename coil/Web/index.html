Coil

How to play

  • Drag through neighbouring tiles — up, down, left or right — to spell a word. Let go to lock it in.
  • The boxes under the board show how long each hidden word is. Every tile belongs to exactly one word.
  • Duel: solve a board and send it. Your opponent plays the same board against your time. Fastest wins the round, then they serve the next board back to you.
  • First to 3 rounds wins. A hint costs 15 seconds. Once you start a round the clock keeps running, even if you leave.
