Wend

Drag across tiles to spell a word

Puzzle

Practice puzzles are unlimited — a new one is made every time.

How to play

  • Drag across neighbouring tiles — up, down, left or right, never diagonally — to trace a word.
  • Let go and it fills the slot with that many letters. Only the correct word gets a green check and locks in.
  • Every open tile is used exactly once. No overlaps, no leftovers.
  • Not enough letters yet? Just pick up where you left off and keep dragging. Drag back over the last tile to undo a letter.
  • Wrong word? Tap it to remove it, drag from its last tile to extend it, or trace a new one over it.
  • Prefer tapping? Tap tiles one by one, then tap the last tile again to place it.
