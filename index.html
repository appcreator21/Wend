Wend

Use every tile exactly once.

0:00
 

Fan-made practice game inspired by LinkedIn's Wend. Not affiliated with LinkedIn.

How to play

  • Drag (or tap) neighbouring tiles — up, down, left or right, never diagonally — to trace a word.
  • The slots under the grid show how many words to find and how long each is.
  • Every open tile must be used exactly once. No overlaps, no leftovers.
  • When you let go, a real word of a free length locks in. Tap a placed word to take it back.
  • Tapping: tap tiles to build a path, then tap the last tile again (or press Enter) to submit.
