Wend

Drag across tiles to spell a word

How to play
  • Drag across neighbouring tiles — up, down, left or right, never diagonally — and let go to place the word.
  • It fills the slot with that many letters. Only the correct word gets a green check and locks in.
  • Every open tile is used exactly once. No overlaps, no leftovers.
  • Wrong word? Tap it (on the board or in its slot) to remove it, or just trace a new one over it.
  • Prefer tapping? Tap tiles one by one, then tap the last tile again to place it.

Fan-made practice game inspired by LinkedIn's Wend. Not affiliated with LinkedIn.
